# Accessibility Statement for TokenRelay

TokenRelay is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards.

## Measures to Support Accessibility
* **Semantic HTML:** The TokenRelay Control Center and marketing surfaces use clean, semantic HTML5 elements.
* **Keyboard Navigation:** All interactive elements, tabs, sliders, and buttons are fully operable via keyboard navigation.
* **Screen Reader Compatibility:** ARIA live regions and attributes (`role="status"`, `aria-label`) are used for real-time toast updates and status feeds.
* **High-Contrast Design:** Color palettes adhere to WCAG 2.1 AA standards for text contrast ratios.
* **Reduced Motion:** Interactive styles respect the `prefers-reduced-motion` media query.

## Feedback & Reporting Barriers
We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of TokenRelay. If you encounter accessibility barriers, please contact us at [accessibility@tokenrelay.dev](mailto:accessibility@tokenrelay.dev) or open a GitHub issue.
